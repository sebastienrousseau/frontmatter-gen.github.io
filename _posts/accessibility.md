Frontmatter Gen is committed to digital accessibility, conforming strictly to **WCAG 2.2 Level AAA** guidelines:

- **Contrast Ratios:** All 62 foreground/background token pairs across Light Mode and Dark Mode are mathematically verified by `audit/contrast.py` and `scripts/audit-contrast.py` to exceed the 7.0:1 AAA threshold for normal text (16.26:1 primary ink in Light Mode, 17.17:1 in Dark Mode).
- **Keyboard Navigation:** Full keyboard operability with visible focus rings, skip-to-content links, and `Cmd/Ctrl + K` search shortcuts.
- **Screen Reader Support:** Semantic HTML5 landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `article`, `footer`) and ARIA live regions across search and FAQ filters.
