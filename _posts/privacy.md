**Last updated:** 31 October 2026

Frontmatter Gen is built on a strict **zero-telemetry, zero-cookie** architecture:

1. **No External Data Transmission:** The `frontmatter-gen` library and `fmg` CLI execute entirely in local memory on your infrastructure. No document content, frontmatter fields, or usage telemetry are ever collected or transmitted.
2. **No Tracking Cookies:** This documentation website (`https://frontmatter-gen.com/`) does not use tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or third-party behavioral scripts. Only your chosen colour theme (`light`, `dark`, or `system`) is stored locally in `localStorage`.
3. **Data Protection Compliance:** Fully compliant with GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
