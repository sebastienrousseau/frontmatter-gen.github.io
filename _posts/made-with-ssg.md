This site is natively compiled using the high-performance **[Static Site Generator (SSG)](https://static-site-generator.com/)** written in Rust and styled with **[Skeletonic CSS](https://skeletonic.com/)**.

- **Source of Truth:** Content authored in Markdown (`_posts/*.md`) with frontmatter headers and Tera/Handlebars templates (`_layouts/*.html`).
- **Compilation Speed:** Full site generation in under 0.5 seconds with zero runtime JavaScript framework dependencies.
- **Security & Provenance:** Strict Subresource Integrity (SRI), Content Security Policy (CSP) headers, CycloneDX SBOM (`sbom.cdx.json`), and `llms.txt` / `llms-full.txt` bundles.
